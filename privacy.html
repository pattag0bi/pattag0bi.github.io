Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy covers the Pattagobi app: a private publishing tool that the Pattagobi channel uses to post its own videos. It is run by the channel owner on their own computer.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the app asks Google for permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, scope youtube.upload), and asks Meta for permission to publish reels to the owner's Instagram account (Instagram Graph API). It uses these permissions only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and captions that the owner has made and approved.

What the app stores

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file only that user account can read. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any viewer or other user.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Meta and Instagram

Instagram access follows the Meta Privacy Policy and Meta Platform Terms. Access can be removed in Instagram settings under Apps and websites, or in Facebook settings under Business integrations.

Deleting data

To remove the app's stored tokens, revoke access as above; the owner can also delete the token file at any time. For any data request, email majorfifahd@gmail.com and it will be answered within 30 days.
